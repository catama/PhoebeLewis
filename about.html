About

You are invited to celebrate the wedding of Phoebe Thomson and Lewis Blomfield on Saturday the 3rd of May 2025.


Please join us at the beautiful Brockwell Park Community Greenhouses (Brockwell Hall, Brockwell Park, London SE24 9BN) from 6-11pm for drinks, dancing and a light supper.


Food

We will be serving welcome drinks from 6, and food from 7. There will be speeches at 8, and then partying until 11!


We will serve a light vegetarian supper of wraps and canapés from En Root, as well as homemade puddings. There will be gluten free, vegan and halal food served.


Drink

We will be serving a selection of wine, beer and soft drinks.
